My Classroom

Posted by a student of the class · School blog

Hello, everyone! Today I want to introduce my classroom. It is a bright and friendly place. There are 30 students in my class and there are 30 desks. Every student has a place to sit, learn and share ideas.

Our class rules

We have 30 class rules, one for every desk. Tap the desks at the top of the page to read them all! The most important one is to keep quiet when the teacher or a friend is speaking. When we listen carefully, everybody can learn better and the classroom stays calm.

Where supplies are kept

We keep our books, notebooks and pencils in our desks and bags. Shared things, like colored pens and paper, are kept in one place so we can find them easily.

Group work activity

We often work in small groups. We talk about a topic together, make posters or flashcards, and then present our work to the class.

Technology we use

Our teachers use a classroom screen to show videos, pictures and presentations. Sometimes we use computers to write and to search for information.

Why it's special

My classroom is special because we have wonderful teachers and kind classmates. Everyone shares ideas, and we help each other. It is a nice place to learn and to have fun.
